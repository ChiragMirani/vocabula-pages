Vocabula

Vocabulary flashcards for English, Math and Science, Kindergarten through Grade 12, on iPhone and iPad.

Get Vocabula on the App Store

Free to download. Ten definitions are open; one $4.99 purchase unlocks every card. No subscription.

Requires iOS 17 or later.

A term on the front, a plain-English definition on the back, written for a student in the grade that card belongs to. Tap to see the meaning, tap again for the next word.

What is inside

  • English — 1,963 words.
  • Science — 1,464 terms.
  • Math — 814 terms, the language of the problem rather than the arithmetic.

Thirteen grades in each subject, 4,241 cards in total. Pick the subject, pick the grade, and study that deck.

What it does not do

  • No quizzes, scores, streaks or badges.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics and no tracking. The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected.
  • No internet needed to study: every card is built into the app.
